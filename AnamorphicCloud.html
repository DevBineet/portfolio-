HelpEvery 30 frames get their own cloud (30 camera views each). Put video.mp4 next to this page and it is processed automatically (or use Upload video), then press Play; its audio plays in sync. Processing the whole video takes a while, so try a short length first. Drag inside the Cloud state or Camera location box to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset zoom. Interrupting playback by touching the Cloud state box pauses it; then pressing Play or moving the slider makes the camera glide to that frame instead of jumping. The Source video box shows the original video when one is loaded (video.mp4 next to this page is loaded automatically, or use Upload video).
